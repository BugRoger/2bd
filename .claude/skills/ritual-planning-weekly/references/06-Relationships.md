## Agent

## Process

1. Gather context:
   - 1:1 meetings scheduled this week (from calendar)
   - People files for each person (`02_Areas/People/*.md`)
   - Follow-ups from last conversations
   - Birthdays/anniversaries this week

2. Interactive:
   - Ask: "Any relationships need attention this week?"
   - Ask: "What do you want to accomplish in each 1:1?"

3. Present:
   - Group by person
   - Lead with context, not agenda
   - Suggest conversation starters
