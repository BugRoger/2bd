# 1-3-5 Method

A daily prioritization framework that constrains focus.

## Structure

- **1 Big** — The one thing that would make today a win
- **3 Medium** — Supporting tasks that move things forward
- **5 Small** — Quick wins and maintenance

## Rules

1. Big must connect to weekly Major Move
2. Medium tasks support the Big or other Major Moves
3. Small are truly small (< 15 min each)
4. Total should be achievable in a realistic day
