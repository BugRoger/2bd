2bd is a personal operating system that helps you lead with intention. It combines an AI executive assistant (Ada) with a structured vault of notes to help you plan, reflect, and grow.

## The Problem

Leadership is overwhelming. You juggle meetings, projects, people, and your own development. Important things slip through the cracks. Reflection happens rarely, if at all.

## The Solution

2bd gives you Ada, an executive assistant who:

- **Runs your rituals** — Daily planning, weekly reviews, quarterly goal-setting
- **Tracks what matters** — Projects, relationships, achievements, goals
- **Synthesizes context** — Briefs you on what's important before you need it
- **Captures insights** — Turns fleeting thoughts into lasting knowledge

## How It Works

1. **You talk to Ada** — Using slash commands like `/plan` and `/reflect`
2. **Ada coordinates specialists** — Brief, Goals, Calendar, Journal, and more
3. **Everything lives in your vault** — Plain markdown files you own forever
