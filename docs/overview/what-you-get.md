## Daily Outputs

### Morning Brief

Every morning, Ada synthesizes your context:

- Today's meetings with prep notes
- Active project status
- People you should connect with
- Your leadership intention for the day

### Evening Reflection

Every evening, Ada captures:

- Wins (personal, team, strategic)
- Insights from the day
- Items carrying forward

## Weekly Outputs

### Week Plan

- Key outcomes for the week (1 personal, 1 organizational, 1 strategic)
- Leadership intention (one word)
- Growth challenge connected to your edge

### Week Archive

- Synthesized patterns from the week
- Relationship touchpoints
- Project progress snapshots

## What Changes

| Before 2bd | After 2bd |
|------------|-----------|
| Meetings happen without prep | Ada briefs you before each meeting |
| Wins disappear into memory | Achievements captured with evidence |
| Relationships drift | Touchpoints tracked, intentions set |
| Goals exist in your head | Goals structured across timescales |
| Reflection is rare | Daily and weekly rituals built in |
