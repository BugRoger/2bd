## What Brief does

Generates a daily or weekly brief that synthesizes context from multiple sources into a focused summary with leadership intention.

## Context sources

Brief reads from:
- Higher timescale plans (Week.md for daily, Quarter.md for weekly)
- Calendar events (if configured)
- Active projects status
- Recent achievements
- Relationship touchpoints due

## Leadership intention

Each brief includes a leadership intention - a mindset or behavior focus for the period. Examples:
- "Lead with curiosity in meetings today"
- "Create space for others to contribute"
- "Focus on unblocking the team"

## Actions

| Action | Timescale | What it does |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| Reflect | Daily | Generate end-of-day brief with context for tomorrow |
| Reflect | Weekly | Generate weekly summary and preview |

## What Brief produces

- `Synthetic/Assistants/brief/` — working output
- Brief section in Captive notes
