Ada orchestrates seven domain assistants. Each specializes in one area of your leadership system.

## The Assistants

| Assistant | Domain | When It Runs |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| [Brief](/assistants/brief) | Context synthesis | Daily/weekly planning |
| [Goals](/assistants/goals) | Priorities and outcomes | All rituals |
| [Calendar](/assistants/calendar) | Meetings and time | Daily planning |
| [Journal](/assistants/journal) | Thoughts and insights | All rituals |
| [Achievements](/assistants/achievements) | Wins and evidence | Reflection rituals |
| [Relationships](/assistants/relationships) | People connections | Weekly+ rituals |
| [Projects](/assistants/projects) | Work tracking | All rituals |

## How They Work Together

During a ritual, Ada:

1. Gathers context from your vault
2. Calls each relevant assistant
3. Assembles their outputs into your Captive notes
4. Updates your knowledge base

You don't call assistants directly — Ada coordinates them based on the ritual type and timescale.

## Concepts

Each assistant follows specific methodologies:

- **Goals** uses the 1-3-5 method (daily), Major Moves (weekly), Quests (quarterly)
- **Brief** synthesizes across all domains for situational awareness
- **Achievements** captures evidence for performance reviews and growth tracking
