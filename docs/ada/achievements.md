## What Achievements does

Captures wins, accomplishments, and evidence of impact as they happen.

## Why track achievements

- **Performance reviews** — Evidence ready when you need it
- **Confidence** — Remind yourself what you've accomplished
- **Patterns** — See what types of work energize you
- **Growth** — Track skill development over time

## Win categories

Achievements tracks:
- **Deliverables** — Shipped features, completed projects
- **Impact** — Metrics moved, problems solved
- **Recognition** — Praise received, awards
- **Growth** — Skills learned, challenges overcome
- **Leadership** — People helped, teams unblocked

## Actions

| Action | Timescale | What it does |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| Plan | Daily | Prompt for wins from yesterday |
| Plan | Weekly | Review week's achievements |
| Reflect | All | Capture and categorize accomplishments |

## What Achievements produces

- `Synthetic/Assistants/achievements/` — working output
- Achievements section in Periodic notes
- Searchable win archive in Semantic layer
