## What Journal does

Captures thoughts, reflections, and personal insights during planning and reflection rituals.

## Reflection prompts

Journal asks questions like:
- What's on your mind right now?
- What went well? What was hard?
- What would you do differently?
- What are you grateful for?

Prompts adapt based on timescale - daily reflections are lighter, quarterly go deeper.

## Insight extraction

Journal identifies patterns in your reflections:
- Recurring themes
- Emotional patterns
- Energy trends
- Growth areas

These feed into the self-learning system.

## Actions

| Action | Timescale | What it does |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| Plan | Daily | Morning intention or mindset prompt |
| Plan | Weekly | Week-ahead reflection |
| Reflect | All | End-of-period reflection prompts |

## What Journal produces

- `Synthetic/Assistants/journal/` — working output
- Journal section in Captive and Periodic notes
- Observations for self-learning
