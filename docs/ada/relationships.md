## What Relationships does

Tracks people touchpoints, connection intentions, and relationship health.

## Touchpoint tracking

For each person in your network, Relationships tracks:
- Last interaction date
- Interaction frequency target
- Open commitments
- Notes from prior conversations

## Connection intentions

Relationships prompts you to set intentions:
- Who do you want to connect with this week?
- What relationship needs attention?
- Who have you been meaning to reach out to?

## Relationship health

Flags relationships that may need attention:
- Overdue touchpoints (haven't connected in X weeks)
- One-sided interactions (you always initiate)
- Fading connections (decreasing frequency)

## Actions

| Action | Timescale | What it does |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| Plan | Daily | Surface people to connect with today |
| Plan | Weekly | Review relationship intentions |
| Reflect | Daily | Log interactions, update touchpoints |
| Reflect | Weekly | Assess relationship health |

## Integration with People hub

Relationships works with your `✱ People` hub and individual person dossiers in `02_Areas/People/`.

## What Relationships produces

- `Synthetic/Assistants/relationships/` — working output
- Relationship section in Captive notes
- Updates to person dossiers
